<p align="center">
  Institutional review of self-paced learning modules<br />
  Laguna State Polytechnic University · Santa Cruz Campus · College of Computer Studies
</p>

EquipED helps faculty and institutional reviewers assess Self-Paced Learning
Modules (SLMs) against institutional references and rubrics. Generated
evaluations and recommendations are advisory; human review remains authoritative.
